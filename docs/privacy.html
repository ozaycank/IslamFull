Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how IslamFull accesses, uses, stores and, where necessary, transmits information when you use the application.

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Overview

Privacy at a glance

IslamFull is an independently developed Islamic lifestyle application published by Özay Can KIRLI.

IslamFull does not require you to create an IslamFull account. The current release does not include advertising, advertising SDKs or third-party analytics SDKs.

Most application settings and personal-use records are stored locally on your device. Some location-related operations can involve operating-system services as described below.

IslamFull does not sell personal data.

Geocoding

Platform geocoding

IslamFull may use the geocoding capability provided by the device or operating-system platform to convert selected coordinates into a readable city and country name.

The relevant platform provider may process the geocoding request according to its own privacy practices.

If a readable place name cannot be obtained, IslamFull can continue using the valid coordinates and timezone information for supported calculations. In that case, a generic location label may be displayed instead.

The current IslamFull release does not implement an additional direct OpenStreetMap Nominatim fallback.

Location

Location data

IslamFull uses location information for features such as prayer-time calculation, Qibla direction and timezone selection.

When you choose to use automatic device location, IslamFull requests foreground location permission from the operating system. IslamFull does not request continuous background location permission.

You may use manually entered latitude and longitude coordinates instead of granting device-location access.

The selected location may include latitude, longitude, city, country and timezone information. This information may be stored locally using platform-protected storage so that IslamFull can remember your selected prayer location.

Prayer-time and timezone calculations use the selected coordinates within the application. A readable place name may additionally be requested through a geocoding service.

Optional local religious progress

Optional religious progress saving and consent

With your optional permission, IslamFull can save your worship activity records, New Muslim Journey reviewed and visited steps, your last-read Quran verse and Quran bookmarks locally on your device. These records can suggest religious practices or interests, so IslamFull treats them as privacy-sensitive.

Saving is disabled unless you actively enable it in the application's Privacy & Legal screen. You may decline and still read the Quran, use guidance content and prayer calculations, and use other app features. These progress records are not sent to an IslamFull account or third-party analytics service.

Consent scope version: religious-progress-v2. An earlier unversioned permission does not authorize this expanded scope. If a saved permission does not match the current scope version, optional saving stays disabled and older local religious progress records are deleted before you can grant new permission.

You can withdraw permission at any time in Privacy & Legal. Withdrawal stops further optional progress saving and deletes local worship activity, journey progress, last-read verse and bookmarks. It does not clear unrelated preferences, such as theme settings or reader display settings.

Deletion covers data held by the application on the current device. Copies retained by operating-system backup, restore, or keychain mechanisms may be beyond the application's immediate control.

Notifications

Local notifications

If you enable reminders, IslamFull can schedule prayer and Daily Verse notifications on your device.

These notifications are scheduled locally through the operating system. The current release does not require an IslamFull push-notification account or an IslamFull notification server.

Notification permission is controlled by your operating system. On supported Android versions, precise notification timing may also depend on the operating system's exact-alarm access.

You can disable IslamFull notifications through the application settings or through your device settings.

Storage & security

Local storage and security

IslamFull uses operating-system storage facilities, including platform-protected secure storage for certain preferences and records.

The protection available to locally stored information depends partly on your operating system, device security, browser environment where applicable, and backup or restore configuration.

IslamFull is designed to minimize unnecessary transmission of personal-use information. However, no software or storage mechanism can provide an absolute guarantee of security.

Third parties

External services and links

IslamFull may rely on operating-system services for permissions, location, geocoding, notifications and secure storage.

IslamFull may also contain links to external websites, including official religious-information sources. If you choose to open an external link, you leave IslamFull and the destination website or service is governed by its own terms and privacy practices.

IslamFull is not responsible for the privacy practices of independently operated third-party websites or services.

Advertising & analytics

No advertising or third-party analytics in the current release

The current IslamFull release is planned as a free application without advertising, paid subscriptions or advertising SDKs.

The current release also does not include a third-party analytics SDK for building advertising or behavioural user profiles.

If these practices change in a future version, this Privacy Policy and the relevant app-store privacy disclosures will be updated before or together with that change.

Retention

Data retention and deletion

Information stored locally by IslamFull remains subject to the storage behaviour of your device, operating system or browser.

Optional religious progress is deleted from application storage on consent withdrawal or when a previous consent version no longer covers the current scope. Other local information may remain until its feature is reset, application storage is cleared where supported, or the application is removed. Operating-system backup, restore, secure-storage or keychain behaviour may cause some information to persist or be restored independently of IslamFull.

Because the current IslamFull release does not provide an IslamFull user account, there is no IslamFull account database to delete.

Information processed by third-party platform or geocoding services is retained according to those services' own policies.

Your choices

Privacy choices

You may decline device-location permission and use manual coordinates instead.

You may decline or disable notification permission and use IslamFull without notification reminders.

You may decline optional religious progress saving or withdraw that permission at any time in Privacy & Legal. The app will delete the relevant local progress records when you withdraw permission.

You can manage application permissions through the privacy and application settings provided by your operating system.

You may also contact the developer if you have a question about IslamFull's privacy practices.
